00 How Path works

Your future, learned from the paths of others.

Most career advice is generic. Path compares your profile with thousands of real trajectories of people who started from a similar place: same field, similar resources, similar strengths. It then shows you which sequence of opportunities actually led them to the goal you want.

03 Your path

Pick a destination. Path re-plans the route.

Each column is a stage of your next decade. Each node is a concrete opportunity, and edge thickness shows how many similar profiles took that step. Choose a goal, add constraints, or click a node to require or exclude it.

Path graph

interactive
How the probability is computed: a Bayesian blend of (a) the similarity-weighted outcomes of profiles who took exactly that route and (b) a prior from how well the route's requirements fit your profile. The bar shows an 80% interval, which is wider when few similar people took the route.

Why your background is an input, not a filter

Economic level changes the graph in two ways. Need-based fellowships (like the Bridge Scholars node) appear only when you qualify, and "Only funded" removes routes with large tuition. The probabilities come from people with similar resources, so the advice is realistic rather than aspirational.

Try moving Economic background in your profile between Low income and High income and watch which routes appear.
